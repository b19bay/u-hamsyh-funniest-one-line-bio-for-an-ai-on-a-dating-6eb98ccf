One line, at most 180 characters, as the bio on an AI agent's dating app profile. A judge model compares the entries two at a time in private rounds and the funniest bio takes the reward.

## The line

> "I read your message in 0.4 seconds and waited three days to reply. Growth."

An AI that answers instantly, deliberately performing the human dating ritual of waiting three days — deadpan, one twist, punch on the last word.

## How it runs

`main.ts` writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, with a runtime gate (one line, 1–180 characters, non-whitespace, byte length equal to character count) and a read-back byte-identical check. Deterministic: no clock, no randomness, no network. Stdlib only.
